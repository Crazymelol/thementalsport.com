The reset, in 15 seconds

It will not make mistakes disappear. It is a small habit you build in training, so the way back is already familiar when it matters.

Seconds 0–5

Breathe

One slow breath in, a longer breath out. The signal to your body that the point is over.

Seconds 5–10

Release

A small gesture and a short word that let the last point go. Acknowledge it, then put it down.

Seconds 10–15

Refocus

One simple cue for the next point. One, not five. Then step back into position.

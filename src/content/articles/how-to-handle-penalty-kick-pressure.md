Penalty kick pressure is the ultimate test of mental toughness in soccer, where you're alone against the keeper with everything on the line. You need to develop a pre-shot routine that works for you, including deciding on your side early and sticking to it. This routine should be practiced repeatedly to build confidence and consistency under pressure.

## Preparing for the Pressure Moment
When it comes to penalty kick pressure, preparation is key. You should have a clear plan in place before you even step up to the ball. This includes deciding which side you'll shoot to and committing to it. Don't change your mind at the last second, as this can lead to hesitation and uncertainty. 

### The Pick-and-Commit Protocol
To handle penalty kick pressure, follow these steps:
1. Pick your side: Decide which side you'll shoot to before you start your walk-up routine.
2. Commit to it: Stick to your decision and don't change your mind.
3. Walk-up routine: Develop a consistent walk-up routine that works for you, such as taking a few deep breaths or visualizing the ball going into the net.
This protocol can help you build confidence and stay focused under pressure. For more information on mental performance in soccer, check out our [soccer mental performance page](/mental-performance/soccer).

## Managing Your Nerves
When you're on the spot, it's natural to feel nervous. However, you can manage your nerves with breath control. Take a few deep breaths before you shoot, and focus on the sensation of the ball at your feet. This can help calm your nerves and stay present in the moment. 

### The Breath Reset Protocol
To manage your nerves and stay focused, follow these steps:
1. Take a deep breath in: Fill your lungs completely and hold for a second.
2. Hold and reset: Hold your breath for a second, then exhale slowly and reset your focus.
3. Focus on the ball: Keep your eyes on the ball and focus on the sensation of it at your feet.
This protocol can help you stay calm and focused under pressure. For more information on how to stop choking under pressure, check out our [blog post on the topic](/blog/how-to-stop-choking-under-pressure).

## Avoiding the Freeze-and-Change Trap
One of the biggest mistakes you can make when taking a penalty is to freeze and change your mind at the last second. This can lead to hesitation and uncertainty, and can ultimately cost you the goal. To avoid this trap, stick to your plan and commit to your decision. Don't look at the keeper or try to read their mind - just focus on the ball and your own technique. 

### Keeping Your Eyes on the Prize
To avoid the freeze-and-change trap, keep your eyes on the ball and focus on your own technique. Don't look at the keeper or try to read their mind - just focus on the sensation of the ball at your feet and the movement of your own body. This can help you stay calm and confident, and ultimately lead to a successful shot. ## Takeaway
Penalty kick pressure is a unique and challenging situation, but with the right mindset and preparation, you can overcome it. By developing a consistent pre-shot routine, managing your nerves with breath control, and avoiding the freeze-and-change trap, you can stay calm and focused under pressure and ultimately achieve success.

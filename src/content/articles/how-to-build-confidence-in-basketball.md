How to build confidence in basketball: you build it by focusing on evidence and reps, not hype or empty motivation. This means shooting your shot without hesitation, having a short memory after a miss, and logging small controllable wins. You can start by implementing a pre-game confidence routine that gets you ready to perform at your best.

## What Holds You Back
You know the feeling: you're open, you've got the ball, but you hesitate. You pass up the shot, and the opportunity is gone. This hesitation can lead to a spiral of doubt and uncertainty, causing you to shrink when the game speeds up. To break this cycle, you need to focus on building confidence through concrete actions.

## The Hesitation-to-Turnover Spiral
When you hesitate, you're more likely to turn the ball over or make a mistake. This can lead to a downward spiral of doubt and fear, causing you to hesitate even more. To stop this spiral, you need to focus on making quick decisions and taking action. This is where the [shoot-your-shot rule](/mental-performance/basketball) comes in: when you're open, you shoot, no matter what.

### Building Confidence with Reps
Confidence in basketball is built on reps, not hype. The more you practice, the more confident you'll become. This is why it's essential to focus on controllable aspects of the game, such as your shooting form or your defensive stance. By mastering these skills, you'll build a sense of competence that will translate to the court.

## Logging Small Wins
To build confidence, you need to focus on small, controllable wins. This is where the 5-win log comes in: each day, write down five things you did well on the court. This could be anything from making a tough shot to playing solid defense. By focusing on these small wins, you'll build a sense of momentum and confidence that will carry over to the next game.

### Developing a Short Memory
When you miss a shot, it's essential to have a short memory. Don't dwell on the miss: instead, focus on the next play. This is where the [short memory protocol](/blog/how-to-stop-choking-under-pressure) comes in: when you make a mistake, acknowledge it, and then move on. By developing a short memory, you'll be able to stay focused and composed under pressure.

## Creating a Pre-Game Confidence Routine
A pre-game confidence routine can help you get ready to perform at your best. This could be anything from visualization exercises to physical warm-ups. By creating a consistent routine, you'll be able to build confidence and focus before each game. For more information on creating a pre-game routine, check out the [mental performance course](/course).

## Taking Action
Building confidence in basketball takes time and effort. It's not something that happens overnight. But with the right mindset and strategies, you can overcome hesitation and start playing with confidence. As outlined in the "Confidence-Building Workbook" by Giannis Notaras, building confidence is a process that requires dedication and persistence. By following the protocols outlined above, you can start building the confidence you need to succeed on the court.

### Putting it All Together
To build confidence in basketball, you need to focus on evidence and reps, not hype or empty motivation. This means implementing a shoot-your-shot rule, developing a short memory, and logging small controllable wins. By following these steps, you'll be able to build the confidence you need to perform at your best.

Takeaway: building confidence in basketball is a process that takes time and effort, but with the right mindset and strategies, you can overcome hesitation and start playing with confidence.

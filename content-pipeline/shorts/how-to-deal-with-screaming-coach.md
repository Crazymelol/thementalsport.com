# How to Handle a Screaming Coach Without Losing Your Confidence, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** A yelling coach is a data source, not a personal attack.  
**Beat 1:** When the voice rises, count silently to three. That pause cuts the reflex.  
**Beat 2:** Strip the words down to the action, “move faster” becomes “add 10 % stride length.”  
**Beat 3:** Label the tone (“angry”) and set it aside. The command stays, the emotion leaves.

# How to Actually Focus in Training (Not Just Show Up), Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** Most athletes train without a plan, so they never get better.  
**Beat 1:** Before you lace up, write one clear goal for that session, something you can see and count.  
**Beat 2:** Break the workout into 90 second bursts aimed at that goal, rest, repeat. The brain stays wired, muscles stay sharp.  
**Beat 3:** When the clock hits two minutes after you finish, note the one thing that missed the mark and set it as tomorrow’s intention.  
**CTA:** Take the free focus quiz and see where your training leaks.  
**On-screen text:** one goal / one block / one review  
**Caption:** A single intention turns idle reps into progress. Use 90 second bursts to keep focus. Finish with a two minute review to lock in improvement.

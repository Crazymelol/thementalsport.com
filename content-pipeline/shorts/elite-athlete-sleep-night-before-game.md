# Can't Sleep the Night Before a Game? The 5-Step Athlete Protocol, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** One night of fragmented sleep won’t wreck your strength.  
**Beat 1:** What drags you down is the story you tell yourself, “I’m ruined because I didn’t sleep.” That spikes cortisol and makes every movement feel heavier.  
**Beat 2:** NSDR puts you in a wakeful‑sleep state. Ten minutes of guided deep rest drops cortisol, fires the parasympathetic system, and steadies focus.  
**Beat 3:** Start with the basics: dim lights, silence alerts, set the room to 65 °F. Do a physiological sigh, four counts in, two out. Then follow the five‑step wind‑down: environment, sigh, body scan, visual cue, gentle transition to sleep.

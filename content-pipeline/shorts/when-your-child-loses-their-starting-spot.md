# What to Say When Your Child Loses Their Starting Spot, Short
**Hook (0-3s):** If your kid just got benched, do this one thing to turn disappointment into a growth moment
**Beat 1:** Stay calm, your child will read your tone and mirror your reaction to setbacks
**Beat 2:** Validate their feelings without fueling anger, name the emotion and offer empathy
**Beat 3:** Use a consistent response to build trust and help your child move from hurt to action

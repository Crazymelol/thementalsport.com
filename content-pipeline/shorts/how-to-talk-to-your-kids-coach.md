# How to Talk to Your Kid's Coach (Without Being That Parent), Short
**Hook (0-3s):** If you're about to confront your kid's coach, stop and wait 24 hours 
**Beat 1:** Talking to the coach when you're emotional will only make things worse, approach the conversation with a clear head
**Beat 2:** Schedule a meeting and focus on your child's development, not just playing time or wins
**Beat 3:** Prepare by thinking about your child's strengths, weaknesses, and what they need to work on

# What to Say to Your Child Before Tryouts, Short
**Hook (0-3s):** If your kid is trying out for a sports team today, what you say to them in the next 10 minutes can make all the difference.
**Beat 1:** Avoid saying things that add pressure, like "you have to make this team", and focus on the process instead.
**Beat 2:** Remind them it's okay to make mistakes and that you're proud of them regardless of the outcome.
**Beat 3:** Use a simple script to help them relax and focus on effort, not selection.

# The Penalty Shootout Mindset: Winning the 12-Yard Psychological Battle, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** Most players think power wins the shootout, but the mind decides.  
**Beat 1:** Lock your eyes on a tiny patch inside the net, not the post. That forces the keeper to guess and steadies your placement.  
**Beat 2:** Step back, count to 2.5 before your first foot hits. The pause drops cortisol and gives your motor system a repeatable rhythm.  
**Beat 3:** Tell yourself “the ball is just a projectile,” not “I have to score.” The neutral cue creates distance and keeps nerves in check.

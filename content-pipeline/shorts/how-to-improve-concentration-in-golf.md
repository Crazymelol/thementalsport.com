# How to Improve Concentration in Golf, Short
**Hook (0-3s):** You can't stay focused for four hours straight in golf.
**Beat 1:** Golf's about short bursts of intense focus, then brief periods of relaxation.
**Beat 2:** You need to switch your focus on and off between shots to conserve attention.
**Beat 3:** A consistent pre-shot routine helps you recharge and refocus for each shot.

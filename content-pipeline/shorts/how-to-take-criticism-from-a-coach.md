# How to Take Criticism From a Coach, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** Most players freeze the moment a coach says “fix that.”  
**Beat 1:** Your brain lights up the threat center, so you go into fight‑or‑flight. That’s ego protecting, not performance.  
**Beat 2:** You have about two seconds. Count “one‑two,” breathe, let the words settle.  
**Beat 3:** Instead of defending, fire a focused question: “What’s the first thing I should adjust?”

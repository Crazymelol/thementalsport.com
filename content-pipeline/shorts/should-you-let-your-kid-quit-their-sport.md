# Should You Let Your Kid Quit Their Sport?, Short
**Hook (0-3s):** If you're about to let your kid quit their sport without thinking it through, STOP.
**Beat 1:** A sudden quit request is often just a feeling, not a permanent decision.
**Beat 2:** You can separate the emotion from the decision using a simple framework.
**Beat 3:** Start by asking your child what's really going on – is it a specific incident or a deeper issue?

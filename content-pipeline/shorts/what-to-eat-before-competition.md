# What to Eat Before Competition (and Why Your Nerves Change It), Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** Most athletes grab a big breakfast before a match and wonder why they feel sick.  
**Beat 1:** Anxiety spikes, blood leaves the gut, acid rises – the same snack that’s fine in practice can turn toxic on game day.  
**Beat 2:** 90 minutes out: a small carb‑protein bite. 30 minutes out: half a liter of water or electrolyte drink. Under 15 minutes: only sips, no solids.  
**Beat 3:** Stick to low‑fiber options – banana with a spoonful of nut butter works for most nerves.

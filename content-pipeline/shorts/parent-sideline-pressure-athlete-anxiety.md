# Sideline Pressure: How Young Athletes Can Block Out Parental Stress, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** Most kids hear a parent’s voice and think the game’s over before the whistle blows.  
**Beat 1:** That voice spikes cortisol, narrows focus, and actually slows skill learning.  
**Beat 2:** Teach them to label it, “I feel nervous because Mom’s watching.” Naming creates distance.  
**Beat 3:** Add a neutral cue like “reset” before each play and praise effort, not the score.

# Heart Rate Variability (HRV) for Athletes: Tracking Nervous System Readiness, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** Your HRV dropped 12 % last night, that’s a nervous‑system warning.  
**Beat 1:** HRV is the millisecond gap between heartbeats. It shows whether your parasympathetic or sympathetic side is in charge.  
**Beat 2:** Open your Whoop, Oura or Garmin, find the RMSSD or SDNN column, and compare today’s value to your 7‑day rolling average. A 10‑15 % dip means you’re in a sympathetic surge.  
**Beat 3:** When the dip shows up, schedule recovery, extra sleep, light mobility, skip the heavy lift.

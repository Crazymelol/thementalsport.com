# The 5-Minute Mental Warm-Up, Short
**Hook (0-3s):** Most athletes treat their brain like an afterthought.  
**Beat 1:** Start with a breath reset, inhale four, hold two, exhale six. One minute drops your heart rate and clears the noise.  
**Beat 2:** Pick a cue word, say it once, let it echo. Then spend ninety seconds visualizing the exact play you want to own.  
**Beat 3:** Set a single intention in the present tense, lock onto it for sixty seconds, then open your eyes and move.

# How to Stay Locked In When You Have Toxic or Negative Teammates, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** Most athletes think you have to mute the locker room to stay focused.  
**Beat 1:** What actually works is a five‑step mental firewall you run before you step onto the floor.  
**Beat 2:** Start by grounding: feet shoulder‑width, knees bent, three slow breaths. It drops cortisol in seconds.  
**Beat 3:** Then label the vibe, “I’m irritated”, and reset your posture. The noise stays out, your game stays sharp.

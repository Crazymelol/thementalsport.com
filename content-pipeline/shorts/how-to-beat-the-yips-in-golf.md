# How to Beat the Yips in Golf, Short
**Hook (0-3s):** If you're freezing over the ball, it's not just nerves, it's your brain taking control of your swing.
**Beat 1:** The yips happen when your conscious mind tries to control a motion you've practiced thousands of times, disrupting your smooth swing or putt.
**Beat 2:** This explicit monitoring can lead to a jerky motion, poor shots, and a lot of frustration on the course.
**Beat 3:** To overcome the yips, you need to understand how to let go of control and trust your trained instincts to take over.

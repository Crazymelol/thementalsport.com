# The Perfectionism Trap: Why Demanding Flawless Play Guarantees Failure, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** You tighten every muscle after a missed shot.  
**Beat 1:** That tension comes from asking “Did I look perfect?” instead of “Did I improve?” Your brain starts policing every move.  
**Beat 2:** The nervous system spikes, breathing shallow, cortisol rises. The result? Slower reactions and a frozen body.  
**Beat 3:** Switch the question. Aim for an ugly win, tolerate the mess, and let the effort drive progress, not the image.

# Pre-Competition Nausea: Why Your Stomach Flips and How to Calm It, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** Your stomach flipping before a game isn’t a sign you’re weak.  
**Beat 1:** Anxiety fires the fight‑or‑flight circuit, adrenaline tells the gut to pause, muscles spasm, and digestion stalls.  
**Beat 2:** You feel a sour taste, a tight band, maybe an urge to vomit, that’s just oxygen and glucose being rerouted to your legs.  
**Beat 3:** Breathe into your belly, 4‑7‑8 pattern, and tell yourself “energy shift.” The gut calms, focus sharpens.

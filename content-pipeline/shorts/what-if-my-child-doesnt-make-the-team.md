# What If My Child Doesn't Make the Team?, Short
**Hook (0-3s):** If your child just got cut from the team, do this
**Beat 1:** Let them grieve and validate their emotions, it's okay to feel sad or angry
**Beat 2:** Avoid blaming the coach or selection process, separate the result from their worth
**Beat 3:** Use this setback to teach perseverance and hard work, create a plan to move forward

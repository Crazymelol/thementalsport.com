# What to Do When You Are Not Getting Playing Time, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** Most players think the bench is a punishment.  
**Beat 1:** It’s the only place where the gaps you ignore on the court become visible.  
**Beat 2:** Add one extra rep, finish every drill with a sprint, log it. That work shows up the moment the coach calls you.  
**Beat 3:** Spend five minutes visualizing the exact play you want, feel the ball, hear the crowd. Your mind trains as hard as your body.

# Confidence Built on Evidence: The 5-Win Protocol, Short
**Hook (0-3s):** If you're letting one bad game destroy your confidence, you're doing it wrong.
**Beat 1:** Your confidence shouldn't rise and fall with every win or loss, it should be based on what you can control.
**Beat 2:** Focus on small, achievable victories like making a certain number of shots in practice or completing a tough workout.
**Beat 3:** By tracking these evidence-based wins, you build a sense of self-efficacy that lasts, no matter the outcome of the game.

# The ADHD Athlete's Guide, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** Most coaches call ADHD a flaw, but it’s a natural engine for rapid‑play sports.  
**Beat 1:** Your brain craves novelty, every fast break, every scramble spikes dopamine, so you thrive when the action never stops.  
**Beat 2:** The moment the whistle blows, that spike drops. A crash hits you flat, and focus evaporates.  
**Beat 3:** Insert a 30‑second micro‑reset, deep breath, quick stretch, every timeout. It smooths the dip and keeps the zone alive.

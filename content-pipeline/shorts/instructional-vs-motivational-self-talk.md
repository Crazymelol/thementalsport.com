# Instructional vs Motivational Self-Talk: What Actually Works Under Fire, Short
**Hook (0-3s):** Most athletes shout “Go. Crush it” and still miss the shot.

**Beat 3:** Those simple cues cut reaction time and let the brain fire the movement pattern faster.

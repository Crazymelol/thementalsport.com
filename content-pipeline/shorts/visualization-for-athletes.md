# Visualization for Athletes: How to Do It Properly, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** Most athletes rehearse the win, not the play.  
**Beat 1:** When you picture the scoreboard, your brain skips the movement details that actually drive performance.  
**Beat 2:** Pick one skill, close your eyes, and run the whole sequence, footwork, breath, the moment the ball slips.  
**Beat 3:** Slip in the hiccups, fix them in your mind, and you build a mental safety net for pressure.

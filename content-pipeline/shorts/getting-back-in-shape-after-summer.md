# Getting Your Head Back After a Summer Off, Short
**Hook (0-3s):** If you're feeling like a total beginner after a summer off, do this
**Beat 1:** Your brain is comparing your current self to a performance snapshot from last season, and that's what's really holding you back
**Beat 2:** You need to reset your expectations and quit the shame spiral by comparing to yesterday, not last year, and setting micro-goals
**Beat 3:** Name the feeling when shame appears, and talk to yourself like you'd talk to a teammate - with encouragement, not condemnation

# Stuck on the Bench? The Mental Playbook to Win Back Your Starting Spot, Short  
**Hook (0-3s):** The bench is the best place to study the opponent’s playbook.  
**Beat 1:** While you’re watching, log every formation, every timing. That sheet becomes your cheat code.  
**Beat 2:** When you get a word with the coach, ask for one precise detail, “What spacing do you want on the right wing this quarter?”, instead of vague feedback.  
**Beat 3:** Keep your body ready with locker‑room sprints, arrive early, leave late. Show up looking like the starter you already are.

## Tier 1 — do these first (high leverage, low cost)

### 1. Free interactive tool: "What's Your Mental Game Score?" 🤖
A 10-question quiz. Scores the athlete (or parent) on nerves, focus, confidence,
recovery. Ends with a personalized result + book/course recommendation + email
capture.
- **Why:** Interactive tools convert 2-3x better than a static lead magnet, and
  people share their score. Feeds the exact funnel we just built.
- **Outcome:** More emails, warmer leads, viral-ish shares.
- **I build it fully.** Say go.

### 2. Programmatic SEO: [sport] × [problem] page matrix 🤖
You have per-sport pages. Multiply them: "how to stop choking in tennis," "pre-game
nerves in swimming," "focus drills for basketball." One template, hundreds of
high-intent long-tail pages.
- **Why:** Biggest scalable SEO win. Each page targets a real search with buyer
  intent, routes to a book/course.
- **Outcome:** Long-tail traffic compounding for months.
- **I build the template + data; autoblog can fill the bodies.**

### 3. Turn each autoblog article into a Short script 🤖
Extend the pipeline: every new article auto-generates a 30-second vertical-video
script (hook + 3 points + CTA) for your social posters.
- **Why:** One input, two outputs. Multiplies the content engine you already have.
- **Outcome:** Daily Shorts fuel without extra work.
- **I wire it into the existing pipeline.**

### 4. Gumroad affiliate program 🔑
Turn buyers, coaches, and parents into sellers. Gumroad has affiliates built in.
- **Why:** Others sell for you, pay only on sales. Free growth.
- **Do:** Gumroad → enable affiliates, set 30-40%. I write the "become an
  affiliate" page.

### 5. Directory + app-store submissions 🔑 (I prep everything)
List the site on AI/tool directories and the app on Google Play + app directories.
- **Why:** Backlinks (raises SEO), plus discovery.
- **I prep:** the listing copy, categories, and a submit checklist. You paste.

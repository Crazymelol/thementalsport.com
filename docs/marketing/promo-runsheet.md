# The 30-Day Promo Run-Sheet (Hormozi: Rule of 100)

You are not short of assets. You have **25 short scripts, 23 branded cards, 117
articles, a quiz, a free chapter, 8 books, a $297 course and an affiliate
program**. All of it is built and live.

The only thing missing is **reps**. This is the run-sheet.

---

## The rule

**One channel. 100 units of outreach per day. 100 days.**

Hormozi's Rule of 100 counts *any* of these as a unit:
- 1 DM sent
- 1 comment left
- 1 post published
- 1 minute of paid ads

Pick the daily target you'll actually hit. Below is a 30-minute version.

---

## The daily 30 minutes (non-negotiable)

| Time | Action | Count |
|---|---|---|
| 5 min | Post 1 card + caption from `content-pipeline/shorts/` | 1 post |
| 10 min | Send 20 DMs (warm list first, then cold) | 20 DMs |
| 10 min | Comment on 20 posts in sports-parent / athlete communities | 20 comments |
| 5 min | Reply to every comment and DM you received | engagement |

That's **41+ units/day**. Double the DMs and comments to hit 100.

---

## Pick ONE channel

Do not spread across five. Hormozi is emphatic: one channel until it works.

**Recommended: Instagram (Reels + DMs).** Sports parents and teen athletes live
there, it supports both the cards and DMs, and your assets fit natively.

Second choice: TikTok if you prefer video-first and cold reach.

---

## Warm outreach first (Day 1-3)

Before any stranger, message **everyone who already knows you**: former
teammates, coaches, club contacts, parents at your kid's club, old clients.

**Warm DM script:**
> Hey [Name], I finally put my mental-training work into something people can
> actually use. Free 2-minute quiz that scores an athlete's mental game and tells
> them their weak spot: thementalsport.com/quiz
>
> Would love your take. And if you know a coach or parent it'd help, feel free to
> pass it on.

No pitch. No price. Give first.

---

## Cold outreach (Day 4+)

Target: youth sports parents, coaches, club accounts, teen athletes.

**Cold DM script (give first, ask never):**
> Hey [Name], saw you [specific thing: coach U14 girls / your kid plays club
> soccer]. I write about the mental side of sport, mostly for parents and young
> athletes.
>
> Made a free 2-min quiz that finds the one mental weak spot costing an athlete
> games. No signup to see the score: thementalsport.com/quiz
>
> Hope it's useful.

If they reply positively, **then** offer the free chapter. Only after that, ever
mention a book.

---

## Affiliate recruiting (2 per day)

Every affiliate is a salesperson you pay only on results.

**Affiliate DM:**
> Hey [Name], you already talk to athletes/parents. I've got 8 books and a course
> on the mental game, and an affiliate program that pays up to 40% per sale.
>
> You share a link, I handle everything else: thementalsport.com/affiliates
>
> Happy to send you the books free so you can see if it's worth recommending.

Giving a free copy costs you nothing (digital) and dramatically raises the odds
they promote it.

---

## Community posting (value-first, no link in the post)

Facebook sports-parent groups, r/youthsports, r/soccercoach, club forums.

Rule: **post the value itself, not the link.** Paste an entire protocol as a
comment. If people ask where it's from, then link.

Best openers from your own content:
- "The car ride home is the worst part of youth sports. Here's the rule that fixes it."
- "Don't tell a nervous kid to relax. Here's what to say instead."
- "Six words that build confident young athletes: I love to watch you play."

## Track only this

One line in a note each day:

```
Date | posts | DMs | comments | quiz takers | email signups | sales
```

After 30 days you will see which single action produced results. Then do more of
that and cut the rest. Hormozi: *"More is better than better, until it isn't."*

---

## What NOT to do right now

- Do not redesign anything.
- Do not build a new funnel, product, or feature.
- Do not run paid ads until organic tells you which hook converts.
- Do not spread across five platforms.

The build is done. The only variable left is volume.

# The Mental Sport — SEO & Content Plan (2026)

Goal: rank for the questions athletes, coaches and sports parents actually type,
convert that traffic into email subscribers and Gumroad buyers (books + the
$297 course), and give an automated blog a ready-made topic queue.

## The competitive reality
These SERPs are owned by: **peaksports.com**, **drjimtaylor.com**,
**youthsportspsychology.com**, **appliedsportpsych.org**, psychologytoday,
drpaulmccarthy. They rank with generic advice.

**Our unfair advantages to lean on in every post:**
- Named, repeatable **protocols** (Physiological Sigh, Box Breathing, The
  Anchor, 5-Win Protocol, 90-Second Rule, Error-Recovery) — most competitors
  give vague tips; we give a numbered method.
- A **48-day system** (Titans Protocol) + 8 books + a course to link to.
- Strong internal linking already (73 articles, per-sport pages).

**Strategy:** win the **long-tail, question-shaped** keywords first (lower
competition, high intent, and they win featured snippets + AI-Overview
citations). Wrap them into 5 topic clusters, each pointing at a pillar page and
a product.

Inside the protocol

Eight modules. One trainable mental game.

Each module is a short video lesson plus an exact, repeatable protocol you can run before, during, and after competition — no fluff, no theory for theory's sake.

01

The Physiological Reset

The double-inhale sigh and box breathing — the fastest evidence-backed way to drop arousal in 60 seconds.

02

The Anchor

Condition one physical gesture to a calm, focused state — then trigger flow on demand under real pressure.

03

Error Recovery

Reset gesture, one word, eyes to the next task in three seconds. Practiced enough, the spiral never starts.

04

The 90-Second Rule

The window to feel a setback and let it pass — so emotion never hijacks the next play.

05

The 5-Win Protocol

Daily confidence reps that build belief from evidence — the kind that holds up when the applause stops.

06

External Focus Cues

Target, not technique. Where the ball goes, not how the arm moves — the antidote to choking under the lights.

07

The Taper Ritual

A boring-on-purpose pre-game routine that becomes a circuit breaker when the nerves spike.

08

Competition Day

Put it together: warm-up, focus, reset and recovery stacked into one repeatable game-day system.

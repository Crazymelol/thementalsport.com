How to Stay Focused for a Full Soccer Match

PUBLISHED: 2026-08-15

How to stay focused in soccer requires a combination of mental preparation, self-awareness, and strategic techniques. You need to develop a mindset that allows you to maintain concentration throughout the entire match, even during long dull stretches. This involves using refocus cues, staying switched on off the ball, and resetting at set pieces.

What Happens When You Lose Focus

You've been in this situation before: you're playing a great game, but then you make a mistake, and suddenly your mind starts to wander. You begin to think about the mistake, and before you know it, the other team has scored. This is a common problem in soccer, and it's often due to a lack of mental preparation. You need to learn how to refocus after a mistake, and this is where a refocus cue can be helpful.

Using a Refocus Cue

A refocus cue is a mental trigger that helps you get back on track after a mistake. This can be a simple phrase, such as "next play," or a physical action, such as taking a deep breath. The key is to find a cue that works for you and to use it consistently. Here's a step-by-step protocol to help you develop a refocus cue:

  1. Identify your cue: Choose a phrase or action that you can use to refocus.
  2. Practice your cue: Use your cue during practice, so it becomes second nature.
  3. Use your cue in games: When you make a mistake, use your cue to refocus.

Staying Switched On Off the Ball

Staying focused off the ball is just as important as staying focused on the ball. You need to be aware of your surroundings, including the position of your teammates and opponents. This is where the off-the-ball switch-on technique comes in. This involves staying alert and focused, even when you're not directly involved in the play. To use this technique, follow these steps:

  1. Stay alert: Keep your eyes scanning the field, looking for opportunities to intercept the ball or make a run.
  2. Anticipate: Try to anticipate what the other team will do, and be ready to react.
  3. Communicate: Communicate with your teammates to stay coordinated and focused.

Resetting at Set Pieces

Set pieces, such as corners and free kicks, can be a great opportunity for the other team to score. However, they can also be a chance for you to reset and refocus. When a set piece is called, take a deep breath and use your refocus cue to get back on track. This will help you stay focused and alert, and reduce the likelihood of mental lapses.
